Fine Print

Find the clauses that cost you money, before you sign.

Paste a client contract or drop in the PDF. A small AI model running in this tab reads every clause and marks the ones freelancers regret: non-competes, IP grabs, no cap on what you could owe, getting paid in 90 days. For each one you get what it means and what to ask for instead.

🔒 The contract never leaves this device. There is no server to send it to.

The reader is EmbeddingGemma, Google's open-weight model, with a small classifier on top trained on 350 real contracts that lawyers had marked up. The first scan downloads it, 197 MB, once.
