ClauseGuard

Checks the contract on this page for clauses that cost freelancers money.

Works on Google Docs, on DocuSign when it shows pages as pictures, and on any page where you select the contract's text. The text goes to ClauseGuard in a new tab on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Not legal advice. ClauseGuard points at clauses worth a second look. It can miss things and it can be wrong.
