INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Independent Contractor Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of March 3, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Brightwater Beverage Co., Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and Jordan Lee, an individual (the "Contractor").

1. Services

1.1 The Contractor will provide brand identity and packaging design services as described in Schedule A (the "Services"), including a logo, a label system for the Company's sparkling water range, and related marketing assets (together, the "Deliverables").

1.2 The Contractor will provide such revisions to the Deliverables as the Company may request, with unlimited revisions included in the fees set out below, until the Company approves each Deliverable in writing.

1.3 The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Company. The Contractor is responsible for all taxes on amounts paid under this Agreement.

2. Fees and Payment

2.1 The Company will pay the Contractor a fixed fee of USD 14,000 for the Services, payable in two installments: 40% on approval of the initial concepts and 60% on final acceptance of all Deliverables.

2.2 Deliverables will be deemed accepted only when completed to the Company's sole satisfaction.

2.3 The Contractor will invoice the Company after each payment milestone. The Company will pay each undisputed invoice within ninety (90) days of receipt of a correct invoice.

2.4 The Company will reimburse reasonable pre-approved expenses, provided receipts are submitted within thirty (30) days of the expense being incurred.

3. Term and Termination

3.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months. Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

3.2 The Company may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason, upon five (5) days' written notice to the Contractor. In that event the Company will pay only for Deliverables accepted before the date of termination.

3.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days of written notice.

4. Ownership of Work Product

4.1 All Deliverables, and all drafts, concepts, sketches and other materials created by the Contractor in connection with the Services, are works made for hire. To the extent any of them is not a work made for hire, the Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in and to them, including all intellectual property rights, effective upon creation.

4.2 The assignment in Section 4.1 also covers any pre-existing materials, templates, fonts, tools or methods of the Contractor that are incorporated into the Deliverables.

4.3 The Contractor shall not, at any time, contest or assist any third party in contesting the Company's ownership of the Deliverables or any intellectual property rights in them.

4.4 The Contractor may not display the Deliverables in any portfolio or publication without the Company's prior written consent.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 The Contractor will keep confidential all non-public information of the Company disclosed in connection with this Agreement, and will use it only to perform the Services. This obligation survives for three (3) years after this Agreement ends.

6. Restrictive Covenants

6.1 During the term of this Agreement and for eighteen (18) months thereafter, the Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, provide design or branding services to any business that produces or sells beverages anywhere in North America.

6.2 During the term of this Agreement, the Contractor will provide branding and packaging design services exclusively to the Company and will not accept engagements for similar services from any other client without the Company's prior written approval.

6.3 For a period of two (2) years after this Agreement ends, the Contractor shall not solicit, or accept work from, any customer, supplier or distributor of the Company, and shall not solicit or hire any employee of the Company.

6.4 The Contractor shall not make any statement, in public or in private, that disparages the Company, its products, or its officers or employees.

7. Delivery and Late Delivery

7.1 The Contractor will deliver each Deliverable by the dates in Schedule A. Time is of the essence.

7.2 If any Deliverable is delivered late, the Contractor shall pay the Company, as liquidated damages and not as a penalty, an amount equal to five percent (5%) of the total fee for each day of delay.

8. Liability and Indemnity

8.1 The Contractor shall indemnify and hold harmless the Company, its affiliates, officers and employees from and against any and all claims, losses, damages and expenses, including attorneys' fees, arising out of or relating to the Services or the Deliverables.

8.2 The Contractor's liability under Section 8.1 and for any breach of Sections 4, 5 or 6 shall be unlimited and shall not be subject to any limitation of liability in this Agreement.

8.3 In no event shall the Company's aggregate liability to the Contractor under this Agreement exceed the fees paid to the Contractor in the one (1) month preceding the claim.

9. Insurance

9.1 The Contractor shall maintain, at the Contractor's own cost, professional liability insurance with limits of not less than USD 2,000,000 per claim throughout the term and for two (2) years afterwards, and shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

10. Records

10.1 The Contractor shall keep accurate records of time spent and expenses incurred, and the Company or its auditors may inspect and audit those records, and the Contractor's related books and accounts, at any time on reasonable notice.

11. General

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.

11.2 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and sent to the addresses set out above, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

11.3 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties about its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions. It may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.

11.4 If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the rest of it will remain in effect.

11.5 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

Signed by the parties on the Effective Date.
